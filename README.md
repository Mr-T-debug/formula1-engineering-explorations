# formula1-engineering-explorations

Independent engineering investigations exploring the technical challenges of Formula 1 through mathematical modelling, simulation and computational analysis.

Formula 1 represents some of the most challenging applications of engineering, where teams chase minute improvements in efficiency, poerformance, and reliability to stay ahead of the competition. This has become even more important as of recent, since regulation changes give rise to upgrades that could bring significant performance enhancements that can make a team with the fourth fastest car become the quickest in the space of just one weekend. 

This repository is a collection of independent engineering investigations exploring the technical challenges faced in Formula 1. Through mathematical modelling, numerical simulations and computational analysis, I aim to better understand the engineering principles behind the sport and how they influence vehicle performance. 

The projects are driven by curiosity and a desire to explore interesting engineering questions, ranging from power-unit performance and energy management to aerodynamics, vehicle dynamics, transmissions and control systems. I have been a fan of Formula 1 ever since I was 5, and aim to one day fulfil my goal of reaching the pinnacle of motorsport. 

Rather than simply explaining engineering concepts, the aim is to investigate them quantitatively, understand the underlying physics and explore the trade-offs involved in engineering design.
